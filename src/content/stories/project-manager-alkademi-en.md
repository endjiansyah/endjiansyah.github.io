Every career trajectory features pivotal turning points. For me, that moment arrived in early 2024. Following the completion of an intensive bootcamp cohort at Alkademi, an unexpected vote of confidence emerged: I was appointed as a **Project Manager (PM)** at Alkademi. This trust stemmed from the leadership style, proactive communication, and problem-solving orientation demonstrated throughout the bootcamp.

Transitioning from a bootcamp participant to stewarding technology projects was no trivial leap. Yet across this journey, my foundation in technical leadership and project execution was forged directly on the frontlines.

---

### Navigating Without a Senior PM

Entering the role presented a distinct institutional circumstance: there was **no Senior PM** at Alkademi at the time. There was no intermediate managerial tier to provide routine operational guidance.

What initially seemed like a steep climb rapidly transformed into an invaluable learning accelerator: I received direct mentorship from **Mas Alfan, Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Alkademi**.

![Endjiansyah facilitating team coordination and technical alignment at Alkademi](/assets/img/stories/project-manager-alkademi/endjiansyah-project-manager-alkademi.webp)
*Endjiansyah leading an engineering coordination session at Alkademi, fostering open communication and technical alignment.*

Learning directly under executive engineering leadership instilled a comprehensive, high-level perspective:
1. **Bridging Stakeholder Vision and Technical Backlogs**: Translating partner objectives and user needs into clear, actionable architectural tasks for the engineering team.
2. **Developer Sprint Cadence**: Balancing team throughput to maintain delivery momentum without compromising code health or causing burnout.
3. **Proactive Risk Mitigation**: Handling unpredictable technical blockers and deployment hurdles with structured problem-solving and decisive execution.

---

### The Round Table: The Entire 2024 Alkademi Developer Team

Among the most enduring memories of daily life as a PM are the collaborative sessions held around the circular table at Alkademi's office. This was where the entire 2024 Alkademi developer cohort gathered with laptops open for daily standups, sprint retrospectives, and architecture brainstorming.

The team formation around the round table held a unique dynamic:
- **Mas Robet and Mas Alif**: Senior developers at Alkademi who had mentored our cohort during the bootcamp the previous year.
- **Adnan, Farid, Hanif, Maahir, and Rijal**: Their juniors who were also my fellow bootcamp peers from the Far Capital program.

### The Common Thread Across Diverse Initiatives

Reviewing the wider spectrum of initiatives in this portfolio—from regional platforms with the **ASEAN Foundation**, healthcare and social aid systems for **AWS in Communities in Karawang**, AI literacy piloting with **Minecraft Education** reviewed by VP Gibran, educator empowerment in **Microsoft Elevate**, to community hackathons like **Lailatul Coding**—a shared foundation becomes evident.

Each of these endeavors was anchored in my role as Project Manager: orchestrating technical talent, aligning client timelines, managing cross-sector stakeholder expectations, and ensuring that every delivered system created tangible value.

---

### February 2026 Dynamics: Passing the Baton and Reinforcing Engineering Frontlines

Time progressed, and by early 2026, Alkademi had scaled substantially. The organizational structure had matured with a growing roster of dedicated talent across project management operations.

With this evolution, the operational baton of Project Manager was handed over to **Mas Hanif Fajri**, who now stewards project deliveries across Alkademi.

Simultaneously, the organization experienced a surge in strategic demand for specialized technical curriculum authoring, module development, and direct software engineering. Because most project management colleagues were dedicated to operational and non-technical domains, having leadership with deep, hands-on architectural understanding at the engineering front became essential.

Recognizing this strategic priority, I made the conscious transition to **refocus my energy back onto Software Engineering**. Working alongside the core engineering team—**Adnan, Farid, Hanif, and Maahir**—my focus is now dedicated to technical module design, engineering mentorship, and robust system architecture. Furthermore, the partnership with Mas Hanif Fajri remains seamlessly collaborative; on topics requiring deep technical deliberations and architectural advisory, we continue to sit together to formulate the best solutions.

This journey confirms that professional roles in tech are fundamentally fluid and adaptive. Whether steering strategy as a Project Manager or architecting systems as a Software Engineer, my core mission remains unchanged: delivering the highest impact wherever the organization needs it most.
