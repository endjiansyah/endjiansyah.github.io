On July 12, 2025, at **Paragon Community Hub, Jakarta**, the inaugural edition of **Festival Belajar Bersama Alkademi 2025** took place. This collaborative gathering brought together various communities—ranging from academic partners like STT Cipasung to industry allies like ParagonCorp and tech enthusiasts—to learn and grow together.

In this event, I had the privilege of serving as part of the **organizing committee as well as a mentor** for the dedicated side-event: the **Kids Corner**. My role was not as a main stage speaker, but rather guiding the children of event attendees so they too could experience computational thinking and creative game design firsthand.

---

### A New Experience: From Players to Creators

Inside the Kids Corner, we welcomed eager children spanning ages **5 to 12 years old**. Naturally, our approach was designed to be playful, visual, and exploratory.

The primary goal was to shift children's perspectives from merely being consumers who play games created by others into proud creators who build their own. Using visual block-based platforms (MakeCode Arcade), they assembled character movements, obstacle interactions, and scoring mechanics for their very own mini arcade games.

![Endjiansyah Alkademi mentor teaching kids computational thinking and game coding at Kids Corner Alkademi Learning Festival Paragon Community Hub](/assets/img/stories/alkademifest-kids-corner-2025/endjiansyah-mengajar-kids-corner.webp)
*Endjiansyah guiding young learners as they build their custom game mechanics during the Kids Corner session.*

Witnessing the smiles and joy when their self-coded games came to life on the screen was a deeply rewarding moment for a mentor.
