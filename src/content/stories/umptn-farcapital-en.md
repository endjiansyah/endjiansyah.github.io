The intensive training program of Alkademi through **UMPTN Farcapital** (Far Capital) became a *pivotal moment* in my career journey as a Software Engineer. Starting with selection in October 2022, this program took me away from home to Bandung for a three-month on-site *bootcamp*.

The early phase consisted of self-learning and online mentoring to align basic technical foundations. But the real acceleration happened when all participants gathered in Bandung to forge their technical skills intensively alongside inspiring mentors and industry practitioners, including valuable guidance from **Sandhika Galih** (Web Programming UNPAS) and senior Alkademi instructors.

---

### Three Months of Technical Acceleration

From November 2022 to January 2023, we underwent a learning process oriented toward real industry practice. 25 participants from diverse backgrounds were forged in a highly dynamic environment.

The first two months focused on mastering *web development* fundamentals up to applying **TALL Stack** (Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, Laravel, and Livewire). At this stage, the main focus was not just making features work, but understanding *clean code*, well-organized architecture, and the performance optimization of modern web applications.

---

### DOJO Project Execution: OCR System

Entering the third month (January 2023), all the material was tested through a final project called **DOJO**. I was entrusted to join the **OCR Team** to develop an automatic text-extraction solution from document images.

The main challenges of this project were extraction accuracy and integrating a responsive backend workflow. A solid team collaboration and mentor guidance were the keys to completing this project on time.

### "Kamar Bersama": The Shared Sanctuary for Decompressing and Fellowship

At the bootcamp dormitory, I shared a room with **Obi Emanuel Pranata**, a resilient colleague from Jayapura, Papua—who later achieved a proud milestone as one of the standout bootcamp participants hired directly by Far Capital.

Although every participant was officially allocated their own bed and room, our room swiftly evolved into a special gathering hub affectionately known as **"Kamar Bersama" (The Shared Room)**. Every night after grueling twelve-hour coding sprints that tested mental endurance, this room became a natural magnet for friends across the dormitory.

For fellow participants, visiting our room was the ultimate remedy for shedding cognitive overload and fatigue after wrestling with complex programming architectures. They sought a safe, welcoming space to share challenges, vent frustrations, or simply laugh out loud while sitting in a circle on the floor playing UNO cards. Serving as a trusted confidant and a source of refreshing camaraderie became one of my most cherished memories—affirming that beyond intensive technical syllabi, human empathy and shared fellowship are the true catalysts that kept our spirits high through to the finish line.
