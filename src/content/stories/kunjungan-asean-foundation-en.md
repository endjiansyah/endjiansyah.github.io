There was a profound sense of pride and gratitude stepping into the **ASEAN Secretariat Heritage Building** in Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta. This landmark is far more than an office—it stands as an enduring symbol of diplomacy, unity, and shared progress across Southeast Asian nations.

On June 17, 2025, alongside the team, I had the honor of visiting the **ASEAN Foundation** office for an in-person coordination and progress meeting regarding our ongoing collaborative digital platform initiatives.

---

### Engineering with Regional Responsibility

Immersed in daily software engineering cycles, sprint reviews, and technical architecture, it is easy to forget the wider human impact behind every line of code. Standing in this historic institution served as a powerful reminder that the solutions we architect hold a meaningful mission to serve communities across Southeast Asia.

Our discussions were both insightful and productive. We reviewed technical development milestones, gathered direct feedback from partners, and aligned strategic roadmaps to ensure that the platform remains robust, scalable, and genuinely impactful for end users.

### Reflections: From Local Foundations to the Regional Stage

Standing beneath the iconic ASEAN emblem flanked by the national flags of Southeast Asian member states was a deeply reflective milestone in my engineering journey. Wearing traditional batik and blangkon within this historic institution brought a distinct sense of pride—bringing local identity and heritage into an international space of diplomacy and collaboration.
