Following the success of the previous year's [Festival Belajar Bersama Alkademi 2025](/en/stories/alkademifest-kids-corner-2025), this annual collaborative learning festival returned with even greater energy in **late July 2026**: **Festival Belajar Bersama Alkademi 2026**.

In this edition, I was once again entrusted with a deeply rewarding responsibility: serving as the **lead mentor in the Kids Corner room**. While the 2025 session focused primarily on young children aged 5–12, the 2026 cohort featured a distinct difference: **a significantly larger turnout of participants spanning elementary school (SD), middle school (SMP), all the way through high school (SMA)**.

### A Multi-Tiered, Dynamic Learning Space

Guiding a classroom with age groups ranging from energetic primary students to analytical high school teens presented a unique, lively dynamic.

We tailored our pedagogical approach across two targeted learning streams:
1. **For Elementary Learners**: Interactive visual discovery through game-based learning. Students explored character navigation and foundational cause-and-effect event handling via Minecraft-inspired environments.
2. **For Middle & High School Students**: Deeper algorithm construction powered by **MakeCode Arcade**. Older students were challenged to architect multi-stage game mechanics, including score tracking, collision detection, and procedural level design.

### Cultivating Computational Thinking Across Generations

With the main projection screen mirroring visual programming blocks to every student's workstation, the diverse age distribution sparked an inspiring peer-learning atmosphere: older students naturally supported younger peers who encountered stumbling blocks in their logic sequences.

Returning to the Kids Corner stage was not merely about teaching game development; it was about sparking authentic curiosity. In these collaborative classroom moments, foundational computational thinking and problem-solving skills take root, nurturing the next generation of Indonesian digital innovators.
